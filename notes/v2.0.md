## Several conversions at once

The panel is now a stack of rows rather than a single pair. Each row has its
own amount, currencies, result, swap and copy, so you can keep DKK → VND and
DKK → EUR in view together instead of switching back and forth between them.

**Working with rows**

- `⌘N`, or the **+** button, adds a row starting on the last row's pair
- **×** removes a row
- Past the height of your smallest screen the stack scrolls
- `⌘S` and `⇧⌘C` act on the row you typed in last

**Upgrading from 1.x**

Your existing amount and currency pair become the first row. Nothing to set up
again.

**Also in this release**

- One rate fetch serves every row, so adding rows costs no extra network
- The ECB rate date moved to a shared footer beneath the stack
- The panel's width now grows with its content instead of being fixed

**Unchanged**

Arithmetic in any amount field (`1250+300*2`), decimals that follow the
currency rather than the size of the number, a universal build for Intel and
Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.

**Install**

Download `CurrencyPop.zip`, unzip, drag `CurrencyPop.app` to `/Applications`.
Signed and notarised, so it opens with a double-click. Look for the ⇄ in your
menu bar — the app has no Dock icon and no window.
