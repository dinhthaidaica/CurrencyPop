## Updates install themselves

The update notice used to hand you the release page and leave the rest —
download, unzip, drag, replace — to you. It now offers **Install & relaunch**:
the app fetches the release it names, swaps itself out and reopens on the new
version, with your rows where you left them.

It is deliberately narrow about what it will accept. The download must carry
the same bundle identifier and the same signing team as the app running it,
and be at least the version its tag claims, or nothing on disk is touched.
Anything tampered with, foreign or older is refused before the swap begins.

The button appears only where the swap can work. A copy in a folder it cannot
write to, Gatekeeper's translocated copy of a zip you never unpacked, and a
build from source all show the download link instead. So does a failed
install, rather than leaving you to retry the same thing. Nothing installs
without your click.

## Vietnamese dong

VND is in the currency list. The European Central Bank does not publish it, so
it comes from [currency-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api),
keyless and CC0 like the ECB feed. While a row shows VND the footer carries
that source's own date rather than the ECB's, since the two are published on
different days. If its fetch fails, the last rate it gave keeps working.

## Amounts with separators

`1.234,56` and `1,234.56` both read as 1234.56. A mark repeated is grouping
(`1.000.000`), and the ambiguous single mark — `1.250` — follows your Mac's
region, while `0.125` is always a decimal. Whenever the field holds a
separator, a `= …` line above it shows the number the app actually read, so a
wrong guess is visible before you trust the result.

## Also in this release

- A result left over from an amount that no longer parses is dimmed, read as
  out of date by VoiceOver, and never copied. `176,12 DKK` used to show, and
  copy, the answer for `176`.
- A failed update check waits an hour instead of asking GitHub again on every
  open.
- The update check no longer queues behind the rate fetch.
- `release.sh` refuses to publish when the bundle version and the tag
  disagree, which is what would have left a release offering itself forever.

## Unchanged

Stacked rows, arithmetic in any amount field, decimals that follow the
currency rather than the size of the number, a universal build for Intel and
Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.

## Install

This is the last one you install by hand. Download `CurrencyPop.zip`, unzip,
drag `CurrencyPop.app` to `/Applications` — from here the notice does it for
you. Signed and notarised, so it opens with a double-click. Look for the ⇄ in
your menu bar; the app has no Dock icon and no window.
